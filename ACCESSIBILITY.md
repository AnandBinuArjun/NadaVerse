# Accessibility Statement for NadaVerse

NadaVerse is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities, students of all ages, and traditional practitioners of Carnatic music worldwide. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

## Measures to Support Accessibility

NadaVerse takes the following measures to ensure accessibility:
- **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance:** Striving for conformity with Level AA standards across all web pages.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** All interactive elements, including metronome controls, audio companion recorders, and quizzes, are fully operable via keyboard (`Tab`, `Space`, `Enter`, Arrow keys).
- **High Contrast & Semantic Palettes:** Styled with curated high-contrast dark-mode palettes (e.g. Amber-400 `#fbbf24`, Emerald-400 `#34d399`, Slate-900 `#0f172a`) meeting contrast ratios >= 4.5:1.
- **Multilingual Transliteration:** Dual-language toggle supporting both Malayalam (`മലയാളം`) and English with standardized diacritics for Carnatic swarasthanas.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Semantic HTML5 elements (`nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `header`), explicit ARIA attributes, and accessible labels on all icons and audio buttons.
- **Non-Auditory Visual Aids:** Real-time visual kriya hand gestures in Tala Studio and pitch contour line charts in the Practice Companion to complement auditory feedback.

## Supported Environments
- Latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, and Microsoft Edge.
- Responsive layouts tested for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewport widths.

## Known Limitations and Feedback
While we strive for comprehensive accessibility, some real-time audio canvas visualizations (such as raw FFT spectrums) rely on HTML5 Canvas. We provide numerical and text-based tabular equivalents for swara deviation cents and stability scores.
