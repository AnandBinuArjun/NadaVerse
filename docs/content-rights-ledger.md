# NadaVerse — Item-Level Content Rights & Provenance Ledger

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 17 October 2026  
**License Policy:** 100% Free & Open Access (CC-BY-SA 4.0 / Public Domain / MIT Code)  
**Governance Standard:** Every published composition, exercise notation, instrument profile, heritage article, and synthesized asset is cataloged with verified primary source documentation, edition rights, offline caching status, and review date.

---

## 1. Principles of Rights & Provenance

1. **Zero Pirated Media:** No proprietary studio master recordings, commercial MP3s, or scanned copyrighted books are hosted or served.
2. **Treatise-Grounded Authenticity:** Every raga definition, swarasthana interval, and tala structure is linked to peer-reviewed classical treatises (*Chaturdandi Prakasika*, *Sangita Ratnakara*, *Sangita Sampradaya Pradarsini*, *Natya Shastra*).
3. **Synthetic Independence:** All auditions (Veena, Violin, Mridangam, Flute, Tambura, Ghatam, Kanjira, Nadaswaram, Morsing) and Tala metronomes are generated locally in real-time via the Web Audio API using mathematical Just Intonation synthesis algorithms under the MIT license.
4. **Quarantine Protocol:** Any asset whose copyright or rights clearance cannot be independently confirmed is immediately quarantined and excluded from public endpoints and offline caches.

## 3. Takedown & Musicological Correction Workflow

NadaVerse provides transparent community and scholarly correction mechanisms:
1. **In-App Suggest Correction:** Every raga card and tutor response includes a "Suggest Correction" action (`/api/v1/corrections`).
2. **Mandatory Peer-Review:** Submissions default to `pending_review` status and are reviewed by musicology editors before any modification is merged into the verified corpus.
3. **Formal Notice Route:** For copyright inquiries or scholarly disputes, email `anandbinuarjun@zohomail.eu` or file an issue with the `rights-takedown` label. Response time is within 48 hours.
