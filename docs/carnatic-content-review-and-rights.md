## 1. Core Licensing & Rights Principles

NadaVerse operates strictly as a **100% free, non-monetized classical educational resource**. To maintain complete legal and musicological integrity:

1. **Zero Copyright Infringement:** No copyrighted modern notations, proprietary commercial sheet music, or unconsented commercial concert recordings are hosted or redistributed.
2. **Public Domain Treatises:** Foundational musicological definitions, raga classifications, and compositions originate from pre-20th-century classical treatises that are in the global public domain.
3. **Explicit Distinction between Synthesis and Live Recordings:** All synthetic audio generated by the Web Audio API engine (Tanpura, Just Intonation swaras, Solkattu metronome strokes) is explicitly tagged as **`synthetic_browser_generated`**.
4. **Peer-Reviewed Editorial Workflow:** All community suggestions and corrections must pass through administrative scholar review prior to public publication.
5. **Notice and Takedown Policy:** A formal DMCA/copyright removal procedure is published with an enforceable 24-hour response SLA.

## 3. Item-Level Content Rights Classification

### 3.1 Raga & Tala Metadata
- **Classification:** Public Domain Facts / Mathematical Ratios.
- **Justification:** Arohana, avarohana, swarasthanas, and tala anga counts (Laghu, Dhrutam, Anudhrutam) are foundational mathematical structures of classical music that are not eligible for copyright protection.
- **Attribution:** Treatises and canonical creators (e.g., Purandara Dasa for the Mayamalavagowla learning scheme; Venkatamakhin for the 72 Melakarta system) are explicitly credited.

### 3.2 Classical Compositions (Kritis & Varnams)
- **Trinity & Pre-Trinity Masters:** Compositions of Purandara Dasa (1484–1564), Tyagaraja (1767–1847), Muthuswami Dikshitar (1775–1835), Syama Sastri (1762–1827), and Swathi Thirunal (1813–1846) are in the global public domain.
- **Notation Editions:** Modern published books containing proprietary commentary or typesetting are **not reproduced verbatim**. Only canonical swara-sahitya skeletons verified against out-of-copyright manuscripts (such as the 1904 *Sangita Sampradaya Pradarsini*) are utilized.

### 3.3 Audio Assets
1. **Web Audio Synthesized Audio (`synthetic_browser_generated`):**
   - Tanpura overtone drones, flute/veena swara audition tones, and Mridangam Solkattu strokes are computed mathematically in the user's browser using HTML5 Web Audio API oscillators, filters, and gain nodes.
   - **Rights:** Created *in situ* by the open-source code; free of third-party copyright.
2. **Curated Listening Library Outbound Links:**
   - NadaVerse embeds verified educational links directly to official archives (e.g., Sangeet Natak Akademi, Library of Congress, or rights-cleared YouTube public domain archives).
   - NadaVerse hosts zero unauthorized audio MP3s on its servers.

## 5. DMCA & Copyright Takedown Procedure

If any copyright holder believes an educational excerpt or user submission infringes their legal rights:

1. **Designated Agent Contact:** `rights@nadaverse.internal` or via GitHub Issue tagged `legal-review`.
2. **Required Information:**
   - Specific URL / entity ID of the contested content.
   - Identification of the copyrighted work and proof of ownership.
   - Contact information of the owner or authorized agent.
3. **Response Protocol:**
   - **Immediate Quarantine:** Contested entry is set to `editorial_status="quarantined"` within **24 hours**, immediately removing it from public view.
   - **Investigation & Resolution:** Editorial board verifies provenance. If valid rights claim, content is permanently deleted and recorded in the audit log.
